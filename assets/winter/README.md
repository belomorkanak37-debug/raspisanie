# Зимние персонажи

Иллюстрации созданы встроенным ImageGen на прозрачном фоне. В приложении используются WebP с сохранённой прозрачностью: `ded-moroz.webp` и `snowman.webp`. Звук не используется.

## Дед Мороз

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG character for an interactive winter scene in a theatre schedule app. A charming Russian Ded Moroz (Father Frost), a handmade felt-and-clay theatre puppet, friendly expressive face, bushy white beard, long red coat with white fur and delicate gold trim, rounded red fur hat (not floppy Santa hat), one mitten raised waving, boots visible, facing the viewer. Single full-body character only, centered, compact silhouette, no props outside the silhouette. Warm storybook lighting and crisp readable shapes at small mobile size. Genuine transparent background, no floor, no shadow plane, no text, no speech bubble, no logo. Square canvas, character fills 90% of canvas height with empty transparent margins.

## Снеговик

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG snowman character for an interactive winter scene in a theatre schedule app. A charming plump snowman as a handmade felt-and-clay theatre puppet, three round white snowballs, carrot nose, tiny black coal eyes and warm smile, knitted teal winter cap and long mustard yellow scarf, two short rounded twig arms with mittens, one mitten waving. Single full-body snowman only, centered, compact silhouette, facing viewer, crisp readable shapes at small mobile size, soft plush texture, warm storybook lighting. Genuine transparent background, no floor, no shadow plane, no scenery, no text, no speech bubble, no logo. Square canvas, character fills 85% of height with empty transparent margins.
